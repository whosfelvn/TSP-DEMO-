# Operational Readiness Transition Support Pack (demo)

Phone-first demo of the Transition Support Pack app. **All names, rosters and figures are fictional sample data.**
Every phone number is 07700 900111, an Ofcom-reserved fictional number, so Call and Copy never ring a real person.

## Try it on iPhone
1. Open the site link in Safari.
2. Tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen like an app and keeps working with weak signal.

Edits you make (notices, roster, images) are saved on your own phone only.
